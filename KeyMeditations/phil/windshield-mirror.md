Nobody owns this one; it circulates through coaching rooms, locker rooms, and sales floors, and its power is in the proportions. The mirror isn't removed — a driver who never checks it is dangerous — it's just sized to the job: quick, deliberate glances backward in service of moving forward. The design of the car is a prescription for attention. Reflection has a place; rumination does not get the whole windshield. Where the Keyes axiom on cognizance asks "do you know where you are?", this one asks "which direction is your glass facing?" — and reminds you the ratio was decided by people who wanted you to survive the trip.
