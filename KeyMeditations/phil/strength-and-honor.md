In the film it's how Maximus and his officers greet and part — two words traded like a handshake before battle. It compresses an entire ethic into a breath: strength, the capability to act; honor, the constraint on how. Either word alone is a warning sign — strength without honor is a warlord, honor without strength is a bystander with good intentions. As a battle cry it works because it's a checklist you can say in one exhale: am I bringing both today? Its natural pair in this deck is the Keyes axiom on what's eternal; love, truth, and honor is the longer creed this salute abbreviates for field use.
