Unattributed, and probably unattributable — this is the kind of line that gets re-derived in every generation because people keep observing the same mechanic: emotional states are contagious, and anxiety about the self is among the most contagious of all. A person at ease signals that nothing here needs managing, and the room relaxes to match. The inverse explains most social friction — discomfort with yourself outsources the discomfort to everyone around you, who can feel the performance without being able to name it. Note what the line doesn't promise: not that everyone will like you, only that they'll be at ease. Downstream of know thyself; you can't be comfortable with a stranger.
