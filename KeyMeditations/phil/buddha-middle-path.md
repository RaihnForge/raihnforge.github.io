The Buddha's authority on this one is biographical: he had run both experiments personally — a prince's palace of indulgence, then years of asceticism severe enough to nearly kill him — and found that both extremes make the same error of letting an extreme do your thinking. The Middle Way of his first sermon is not moderation-as-mediocrity or splitting every difference; it's the recognition that extremes are usually easier than judgment. Total indulgence and total denial both remove the daily decision. The middle path is harder precisely because it must be found again each day, by an awake person. The internal-state axiom's 20–80 band is the same instinct with percentages on it.
