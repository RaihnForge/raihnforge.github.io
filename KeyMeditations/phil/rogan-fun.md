A recurring Rogan refrain rather than a single citable moment, and easy to misread as "optimize for pleasure." The stronger reading is diagnostic: fun is a trailing indicator of a life in working order. Genuine fun — not numbing, not escape — requires present attention, low background dread, people you actually like, and enough command of your obligations that they aren't screaming. If you can still laugh in the middle of hard work, the systems are healthy; if nothing is fun anymore, something upstream is broken, and no achievement will patch it. That makes fun worth monitoring the way you'd monitor sleep. It's the deck's least solemn card and possibly its most honest gauge.
