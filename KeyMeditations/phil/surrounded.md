At the Chosin Reservoir in 1950, the 1st Marine Division was encircled by vastly larger Chinese forces in minus-thirty-degree cold. Puller's remark — variations survive, all in the same key: "We're surrounded. That simplifies our problem" — wasn't denial. The Marines fought their way out with their wounded, their equipment, and the division intact. The reframe is the weapon: the facts of the situation are fixed, but the framing decides what the situation is for. Uncertainty is heavier than bad news; at least the located enemy can be engaged. As a daily card, it's for the moment the problems all arrive at once — good, no more guessing where they are.
