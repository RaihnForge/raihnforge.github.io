A joke that survives contact with philosophy. Dwight's filter is a genuine via negativa — the decision procedure Charlie Munger spent a career preaching as inversion: don't ask what a wise move looks like, ask what a stupid one looks like and don't do that. It works because failure modes are far more predictable than success modes; there are a thousand ways to be clever and about six ways to be an idiot, and the six are well documented. The comedy is that Dwight applies it with total sincerity, which is also the instruction. It earns its place in a wisdom deck the honest way: it's cheap, it's fast, and it catches real errors before they ship.
