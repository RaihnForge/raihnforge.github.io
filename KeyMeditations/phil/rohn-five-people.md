Rohn's "average of five" is a motivational heuristic, not a measurement — but the direction of the claim has real support. The Framingham network studies found that obesity, smoking, and even happiness propagate through social ties, out to friends-of-friends. Norms are contagious; your circle quietly sets your baseline for what's normal effort, normal honesty, normal ambition. The Keyes rewording adds two things Rohn's version lacks: the mirror (your current circle is diagnostic — you chose it) and the trajectory (it bounds who you can become, not just who you are). The actionable edge pairs with the investment axiom: curate deliberately, and invest where investment is met.
